## **1. Mathematical Specification (Code-Ready Extraction)**

### **Governing Equations**
Conservative compressible Euler with gravitational source (gravity in \(+y\)) on \((x,y)\in\Omega\):
\[
\partial_t
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho\\ \rho u\\ \rho v\\ E
\end{pmatrix}
+
\partial_x
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho u\\ \rho u^2 + p\\ \rho u v\\ (E+p)u
\end{pmatrix}
+
\partial_y
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho v\\ \rho u v\\ \rho v^2 + p\\ (E+p)v
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0\\ 0\\ \rho g\\ \rho g v
\end{pmatrix}
\]
with equation of state
\[
p=(\gamma-1)\left(E-\tfrac12\rho(u^2+v^2)\right),\qquad \gamma=1.4,\qquad g=1.0.
\]

### **Domain**
\[
(x,y)\in[0,0.25]\times[0,1.0],\qquad t\in[0,3.0].
\]

### **Boundary Conditions**
- \(x=0,\,0.25\): **Periodic** for all conserved variables.  
- \(y=0,\,1\): **Reflective / free-slip wall**:
  \[
  v(x,0,t)=0,\qquad v(x,1,t)=0,
  \]
  with tangential velocity \(u\) unconstrained (slip), implemented via reflective state in the normal component.

### **Initial Conditions**
Density (smoothed interface at \(y=0.5\)):
\[
\rho(x,y,0)=\rho(y)=2.0+(1.0-2.0)\cdot \frac12\left(1+\tanh\!\big(50(y-0.5)\big)\right).
\]

Pressure (given hydrostatic piecewise profile):
\[
p(x,y,0)=p(y)=
\begin{cases}
2y+1, & y<0.5,\\
y+1.5, & y\ge 0.5.
\end{cases}
\]

Velocities:
\[
u(x,y,0)=0,
\]
\[
c(x,y,0)=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma p(y)}{\rho(y)}},
\]
\[
v(x,y,0)=-0.025\; c(x,y,0)\;\cos(8\pi x)\;\sin(\pi y)^6.
\]

Total energy initialized consistently:
\[
E(x,y,0)=\frac{p(y)}{\gamma-1}+\frac12\rho(y)\left(u(x,y,0)^2+v(x,y,0)^2\right).
\]

### **Constants & Parameters**
\[
\gamma=1.4,\quad g=1.0,\quad \mu=0,\quad k=0.
\]

Hydrostatically supported stratification with a seeded vertical-velocity perturbation triggers Rayleigh–Taylor growth: rising light fluid and falling heavy fluid, steepening density gradients and eventual shocklets/compressible wave activity; dominant observable is the evolving **density interface**.
