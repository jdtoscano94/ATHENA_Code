**1. Problem Statement**  
Solve the 2D inviscid compressible Euler equations with gravity to simulate Rayleigh–Taylor instability on a rectangular domain with periodicity in \(x\) and reflective slip walls in \(y\), from \(t=0\) to \(t=3\).

---

## 1. Mathematical Specification (Code-Ready Extraction)

### Governing Equations
Conservative form with gravitational source in **positive \(y\)**:
\[
\partial_t
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho\\
\rho u\\
\rho v\\
E
\end{pmatrix}
+
\partial_x
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho u\\
\rho u^2 + p\\
\rho u v\\
u(E+p)
\end{pmatrix}
+
\partial_y
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho v\\
\rho u v\\
\rho v^2 + p\\
v(E+p)
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0\\
0\\
\rho g\\
\rho g v
\end{pmatrix}
\]
with equation of state
\[
p = (\gamma-1)\left(E-\tfrac12\rho(u^2+v^2)\right).
\]

### Domain
\[
(x,y)\in[0,0.25]\times[0,1.0],\qquad t\in[0,3.0].
\]

### Boundary Conditions
- \(x=0,\,0.25\): **Periodic** for \(\rho,\rho u,\rho v,E\).
- \(y=0,\,1\): **Reflective / free-slip wall** (no penetration):
  \[
  v=0 \text{ at } y=0,1,
  \]
  implemented as reflective BC on the normal momentum (\(\rho v\)) and even symmetry for tangential momentum (\(\rho u\)).

### Initial Conditions
Constants:
\[
\gamma=1.4,\quad g=1.0,\quad \rho_{\text{heavy}}=2.0,\quad \rho_{\text{light}}=1.0.
\]

Density (smoothed interface at \(y=0.5\)):
\[
\rho(x,y)=\rho(y)= 2.0 + (1.0-2.0)\cdot \frac12\left(1+\tanh\!\left(50(y-0.5)\right)\right).
\]

Pressure (given hydrostatic profile):
\[
p(x,y)=p(y)=
\begin{cases}
2y+1, & y<0.5,\\
y+1.5, & y\ge 0.5.
\end{cases}
\]

Speed of sound:
\[
c(x,y)=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma p(x,y)}{\rho(x,y)}}.
\]

Velocity:
\[
u(x,y)=0,
\]
\[
v(x,y)= -0.025\; c(x,y)\; \cos(8\pi x)\; \sin(\pi y)^6.
\]

Total energy:
\[
E(x,y)=\frac{p(x,y)}{\gamma-1}+\frac12\rho(x,y)\left(u(x,y)^2+v(x,y)^2\right).
\]

### Expected Physics
Heavy fluid below light fluid with gravity in \(+y\) is **unstable**: interface rolls up; compressible effects via acoustic adjustment; shocks are not expected initially but local steepening can occur at later times.

---

## 2. Strategic Methodology

### Can obtain an exact solution or similar?
No. Full 2D compressible Euler with gravity and finite-amplitude RTI has no closed-form solution; characteristics do not yield a tractable global analytic solution in 2D for this configuration.

### Selected ONE best method
**Discontinuous Galerkin Spectral Element Method in 2D plus time**  
Rationale: hyperbolic, compressible, potentially under-resolved interfacial structures; DGSEM handles discontinuities/steep gradients and wall/periodic BCs cleanly while retaining high-order accuracy away from the interface.

**Justification (signal-to-noise):**

- Pressure is comparatively smooth due to near-hydrostatic balance plus acoustic adjustment; it is not the best interface tracker.

Use shock-capturing/limiting driven by a density-based smoothness/gradient indicator.
