## **1. Mathematical Specification (Code-Ready Extraction)**

### **Governing Equations**
Let conservative state \(\mathbf{U} = (\rho,\rho u,\rho v,E)^{T}\). Then
\[
\partial_t \mathbf{U} + \partial_x \mathbf{F}(\mathbf{U}) + \partial_y \mathbf{G}(\mathbf{U}) = \mathbf{S}(\mathbf{U}),
\]
with fluxes
\[
\mathbf{F}=
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho u\\
\rho u^2 + p\\
\rho u v\\
u(E+p)
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\mathbf{G}=
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho v\\
\rho u v\\
\rho v^2 + p\\
v(E+p)
\end{pmatrix},
\]
equation of state
\[
p = (\gamma-1)\left(E - \tfrac12 \rho(u^2+v^2)\right),
\]
and gravity source term (gravity \(+y\)):
\[
\mathbf{S}=
\begin{pmatrix}
0\\
0\\
\rho g\\
\rho g v
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

### **Domain**
\[
(x,y)\in[0,0.25]\times[0,1.0], \qquad t\in[0,3.0].
\]

### **Boundary Conditions**
- **\(x=0\) and \(x=0.25\):** Periodic for all conserved variables.  
- **\(y=0\) and \(y=1\):** Reflective / free-slip wall:
  \[
  v(x,0,t)=0,\quad v(x,1,t)=0,
  \]
  with tangential velocity \(u\) free (slip), and wall reflection applied consistently to the conserved state.

### **Initial Conditions**
Constants:
\[
\rho_{\text{heavy}}=2.0,\quad \rho_{\text{light}}=1.0,\quad \gamma=1.4,\quad g=1.0.
\]

Density (smoothed interface at \(y=0.5\)):
\[
\rho(x,y,0)=\rho(y)= \rho_{\text{heavy}} + (\rho_{\text{light}}-\rho_{\text{heavy}})\cdot \frac12\left(1+\tanh\left(50(y-0.5)\right)\right).
\]

Pressure (given hydrostatic piecewise profile):
\[
p(x,y,0)=p(y)=
\begin{cases}
2y + 1, & y<0.5,\\
y + 1.5, & y\ge 0.5.
\end{cases}
\]

Velocity:
\[
u(x,y,0)=0,
\]
\[
c(x,y,0)=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma\, p(y)}{\rho(y)}},
\]
\[
v(x,y,0)= -0.025 \, c(x,y,0)\, \cos(8\pi x)\, \sin(\pi y)^{6}.
\]

Total energy:
\[
E(x,y,0)=\frac{p(y)}{\gamma-1}+\frac12 \rho(y)\left(u(x,y,0)^2+v(x,y,0)^2\right).
\]

@inline function initial_condition_rti(x, t, equations::CompressibleEulerEquations2D)
    rho_heavy = 2.0
    rho_light = 1.0
    
    rho = rho_heavy + (rho_light - rho_heavy) * 0.5 * (1.0 + tanh(50.0 * (x[2] - 0.5)))
    
    if x[2] < 0.5
        p = 2.0 * x[2] + 1.0
    else
        p = x[2] + 1.5
    end
    
    u_vel = 0.0
    c = sqrt(equations.gamma * p / rho)
    v_vel = -0.025 * c * cos(8.0 * pi * x[1]) * (sin(pi * x[2]))^6
    
    return prim2cons(SVector(rho, u_vel, v_vel, p), equations)
end
