* **Governing Equations:**
For the 2D compressible Euler equations with gravity in the positive \(y\)-direction,
\[
\partial_t
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho \\
\rho u \\
\rho v \\
E
\end{pmatrix}
+
\partial_x
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho u \\
\rho u^2 + p \\
\rho u v \\
u(E+p)
\end{pmatrix}
+
\partial_y
\begin{pmatrix}
\rho v \\
\rho u v \\
\rho v^2 + p \\
v(E+p)
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0 \\
0 \\
\rho g \\
\rho g v
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Equation of state:
\[
p = (\gamma - 1)\left(E - \frac{1}{2}\rho(u^2+v^2)\right).
\]

* **Domain:**
\[
(x,y)\in[0,0.25]\times[0,1.0], \qquad t\in[0,3.0].
\]

  * \(x=0\) and \(x=0.25\): periodic.
  * \(y=0\) and \(y=1\): reflective / free-slip wall, with no penetration:
\[
v=0 \quad \text{at } y=0,1.
\]

* **Initial Conditions:**
Density:
\[
\rho(y)=\rho_{\text{heavy}}+(\rho_{\text{light}}-\rho_{\text{heavy}})\cdot \frac{1}{2}\left(1+\tanh\left(50(y-0.5)\right)\right),
\]
with
\[
\rho_{\text{heavy}}=2.0,\qquad \rho_{\text{light}}=1.0.
\]

Pressure:
\[
p(y)=
\begin{cases}
2y+1, & y<0.5,\\
y+1.5, & y\ge 0.5.
\end{cases}
\]

Horizontal velocity:
\[
u(x,y)=0.
\]

Local sound speed:
\[
c(x,y)=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma\,p(y)}{\rho(y)}}.
\]

Vertical velocity:
\[
v(x,y)=-0.025\,c(x,y)\cos(8\pi x)\sin(\pi y)^6.
\]

Total energy:
\[
E(x,y)=\frac{p(y)}{\gamma-1}+\frac{1}{2}\rho(y)\left(u(x,y)^2+v(x,y)^2\right).
\]

* **Constants & Parameters:**
\[
\gamma=1.4,\qquad g=1.0,\qquad \mu=0,\qquad k=0.
\]
