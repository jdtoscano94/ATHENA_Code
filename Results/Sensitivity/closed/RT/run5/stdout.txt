For the 2D compressible Euler equations with gravity in the positive \(y\)-direction, evolve the conserved variables
\[

\rho v \\
E
\end{bmatrix},

\[

\]

The PDE system is
\[
\partial_t \rho + \partial_x (\rho u) + \partial_y (\rho v) = 0,
\]
\[
\partial_t (\rho u) + \partial_x (\rho u^2 + p) + \partial_y (\rho u v) = 0,
\]
\[
\partial_t (\rho v) + \partial_x (\rho u v) + \partial_y (\rho v^2 + p) = \rho g,
\]
\[
\partial_t E + \partial_x \bigl(u(E+p)\bigr) + \partial_y \bigl(v(E+p)\bigr) = \rho g v.
\]

Equation of state:
\[
p = (\gamma-1)\left(E - \frac{1}{2}\rho(u^2+v^2)\right).
\]

* **Domain:**
\[
(x,y) \in [0,0.25]\times[0,1.0], \qquad t\in[0,3.0].
\]

  * \(x=0\) and \(x=0.25\): Periodic.

\[
v=0,
\]
with mirrored normal momentum and unchanged tangential velocity at the wall.

\[

\[
p(x,y,0)=p(y)=
\begin{cases}
2y+1, & y<0.5,\\[4pt]
y+1.5, & y\ge 0.5.
\end{cases}
\]

Horizontal velocity:
\[
u(x,y,0)=0.
\]

Local sound speed:
\[
c(x,y,0)=\sqrt{\frac{\gamma p(y)}{\rho(y)}}.
\]

Vertical velocity:
\[
v(x,y,0)=-0.025\, c(x,y,0)\,\cos(8\pi x)\,\sin(\pi y)^6.
\]

Total energy:
\[
E(x,y,0)=\frac{p(y)}{\gamma-1}+\frac{1}{2}\rho(y)\left(u(x,y,0)^2+v(x,y,0)^2\right).
\]

\[

\]
\[
\rho_{\text{heavy}}=2.0,\qquad \rho_{\text{light}}=1.0.

Rayleigh-Taylor growth from the perturbed density interface near \(y=0.5\), followed by nonlinear roll-up and compressible buoyancy-driven mixing. Density is the primary structural field.
