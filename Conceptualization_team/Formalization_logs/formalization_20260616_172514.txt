Steady incompressible Navier–Stokes in 2D:

- Continuity:
$$
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{u} = 0,
$$

- Momentum:
$$
(\mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{u} = -\nabla p + \frac{1}{Re}\nabla^2 \mathbf{u},
$$

where \(\mathbf{u}(x,y) = (u(x,y), v(x,y))\) is the velocity, \(p(x,y)\) is the pressure, and \(Re\) is the unknown scalar Reynolds number.

### Domain
- Spatial domain:
$$
(x,y) \in [0,1] \times [0,1].
$$
- Steady-state (no time dependence).

### Boundary Conditions
Velocity prescribed on all four walls:

- Bottom wall \(y=0\) (no-slip):
$$
u(x,0) = 0,\quad v(x,0) = 0,\quad 0 \le x \le 1.
$$

- Left wall \(x=0\) (no-slip):
$$
u(0,y) = 0,\quad v(0,y) = 0,\quad 0 \le y \le 1.
$$

- Right wall \(x=1\) (no-slip):
$$
u(1,y) = 0,\quad v(1,y) = 0,\quad 0 \le y \le 1.
$$

- Top lid \(y=1\) (smoothed moving lid):
$$
u(x,1) = 1 - \frac{\cosh(C_0(x-0.5))}{\cosh(0.5 C_0)},\quad
v(x,1) = 0,\quad 0 \le x \le 1,
$$
with
$$
C_0 = 50.
$$

Pressure is determined up to an additive constant; a reference value (e.g., \(p(0,0)=0\)) can be fixed if needed.

### Initial Conditions
- Not applicable (steady-state problem).

### Physics Parameters
- Unknown:
  - Reynolds number \(Re\) (global scalar parameter to be inferred).
- Known:
  - Lid smoothing parameter: \(C_0 = 50\).
  - Geometry: unit square.
  - No body force term (forcing is zero).

### Reference / Measurement Data
- **File path:**
  - `/users/jdtoscan/data/jdtoscan/LLM/Crunch_LLM/Documents/codes/Data/ldc_Re500.npz`

- **Contents:**
  - `x`: 1D array of x-coordinates of nodes,
  - `y`: 1D array of y-coordinates of nodes,
  - `u`: 1D array of u-velocity values at those nodes,
  - `v`: 1D array of v-velocity values at those nodes,
  - `p`: 1D array of pressure values at those nodes.
  - All arrays have the same length \(N_{\text{data}}\); the points \((x_k,y_k)\) form an **unstructured set of nodes** in \([0,1]^2\).

- **Nature of data:**
  - High-fidelity numerical solution of the steady lid-driven cavity at a nominal Reynolds number near \(Re \approx 500\) (likely exactly 500).
  - Assumed negligible numerical noise.

### Execution Strategy
- **Stage 1: (A) SciC — Forward Solver Cross-Check (Optional but Recommended)**
  - Use a steady Navier–Stokes solver on \([0,1]^2\) with the specified BCs on a suitable mesh.
  - Run at \(Re=500\) to:
    - Verify that the solver’s solution qualitatively matches the data (`u, v, p`) when interpolated to the unstructured nodes \((x_k,y_k)\).
    - Confirm consistency of boundary conditions and nondimensionalization with the dataset.

- **Stage 2: (B) SciML-PINN — Inverse Estimation of \(Re\) from Unstructured Data**
  - Construct a physics-informed neural network that:
    - Represents \(u(x,y)\), \(v(x,y)\), and \(p(x,y)\) over the domain.
    - Treats \(Re\) as a trainable scalar parameter.
  - Use:
    - Interior collocation points throughout \((0,1)\times(0,1)\) to enforce the steady NSE and incompressibility.
    - Boundary collocation points along each wall to enforce the specified velocity BCs.
    - The unstructured data points:
      - At each node \((x_k,y_k)\) from `x[k], y[k]`, enforce agreement between PINN predictions and `u[k], v[k], p[k]`.
  - Train the PINN to jointly satisfy:
    - PDE and divergence-free constraints,
    - Boundary conditions,
    - Data consistency at all unstructured nodes,
    obtaining an estimate \(Re_{\text{PINN}}\) and reconstructed fields.

- **Stage 3: (A) SciC — Forward Verification at Inferred \(Re\)**
  - Run the numerical Navier–Stokes solver with \(Re = Re_{\text{PINN}}\) using the same BCs.
  - Interpolate the solver’s solution to the unstructured coordinates \((x_k,y_k)\).
  - Compare:
    - Solver predictions vs. dataset (`u, v, p`),
    - Solver predictions vs. PINN predictions at all nodes.
  - Use these comparisons to validate the inferred \(Re\) and assess the quality of the PINN solution.

### Validation Strategy
- **Data and model consistency:**
  - Compute errors between:
    - PINN predictions and data (`u, v, p`) at each unstructured node,
    - Forward solver solution at \(Re_{\text{PINN}}\) (interpolated) and the data.
- **Physics consistency:**
  - Evaluate divergence and momentum residuals of the PINN solution at collocation points.
  - Compare flow structure (main and secondary vortices) between PINN and forward solver.
- **Parameter accuracy:**
  - If the dataset is known to correspond to \(Re=500\), directly compare \(Re_{\text{PINN}}\) to 500 and assess relative error.

- **Role:**
