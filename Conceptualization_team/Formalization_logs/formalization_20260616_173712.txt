### Problem Statement
Inverse problem for the 2D steady incompressible Navier–Stokes equations in a lid-driven cavity. The goal is to infer:
1. The unknown Reynolds number \(Re\) (scalar parameter, expected in \([300, 525]\)),
2. The hidden velocity field component \(v(x,y)\),

using only sparse, noisy observations of the \(x\)-velocity component \(u(x,y)\). The governing equations and boundary conditions define the forward physics, and a high-fidelity reference dataset at \(Re=500\) is available for validation and error metrics.

### Equation(s)

Steady, incompressible Navier–Stokes in 2D:

- Incompressibility:
\[
\nabla \cdot \mathbf{u} = 0,
\]
- Momentum:
\[
(\mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{u} = -\nabla p + \frac{1}{Re}\,\nabla^2 \mathbf{u},
\]

with
\[
\mathbf{u}(x,y) = (u(x,y), v(x,y)).
\]

### Domain

Spatial domain:
\[
(x,y) \in [0,1] \times [0,1].
\]

Steady-state (no time dimension).

### Boundary Conditions

Lid-driven cavity with regularized lid velocity profile:

- Top wall (\(y = 1\)), moving lid in \(x\)-direction:
\[
u(x,1) = 1 - \frac{\cosh(C_0(x-0.5))}{\cosh(0.5 C_0)}, \quad v(x,1)=0,
\]
with
\[
C_0 = 50.
\]

- Bottom wall (\(y = 0\)), no-slip:
\[
u(x,0) = 0,\quad v(x,0) = 0.
\]

- Left wall (\(x = 0\)), no-slip:
\[
u(0,y) = 0,\quad v(0,y) = 0.
\]

- Right wall (\(x = 1\)), no-slip:
\[
u(1,y) = 0,\quad v(1,y) = 0.
\]

Critical constraint for methodology: **Do not use exact (hard) boundary imposition for these Dirichlet conditions**. Boundary conditions must be enforced in a soft/penalized manner, not built-in exactly.

Not applicable (steady-state problem).
